Format & Export

What is Video to GIF Studio?

Video to GIF Studio is a free online tool that converts MP4, MOV, AVI and WEBM videos into GIF, WebP or MP4 loops. You can trim the clip, add meme captions, change playback speed, and control file size with five quality levels or a target size cap.

How to convert a video to GIF

  1. Upload a video (MP4, MOV, AVI or WEBM, up to 100MB) or click Try Sample Video.
  2. Press [ and ] to set the start and end of your clip.
  3. Pick a quality level from 1 (smallest file) to 5 (best quality).
  4. Optionally add captions, change speed, or enable Boomerang or Reverse.
  5. Choose GIF, WebP or MP4, click Render & Export, then download.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a video to a GIF?

Upload an MP4, MOV, AVI or WEBM file (up to 100MB), set the in and out points to trim your clip, choose a quality level, then click Render & Export and download your GIF.

What video formats can I convert to GIF?

Video to GIF Studio accepts MP4, MOV, AVI and WEBM files up to 100MB.

How do I make a GIF file smaller?

Lower the quality level (Level 1 is smallest), shorten the clip with the trimmer, or set a target size cap. You can also export as WebP, which is typically much smaller than GIF at similar quality.

What is the difference between GIF, WebP and MP4 loops?

GIF works everywhere but has large files and 256 colors. Animated WebP is much smaller with better color. An MP4 loop is the smallest and highest quality but is a video file, not an image.

Can I add text or captions to my GIF?

Yes. Use the Meme Captions section to add text before exporting.

Can I reverse a GIF or make it boomerang?

Yes. Enable Reverse Playback or Boomerang Loop in the Speed & Loop Effects section, and adjust speed from 0.5x to 2.0x.
